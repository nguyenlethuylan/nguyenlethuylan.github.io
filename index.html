Hello, World! I am a data enthusiast with a strong foundation in life sciences. Currently, I am pursuing my Master's degree in Data Science. I am passionate about uncovering hidden patterns within complex datasets and bridging the analytical gap between biology and machine learning.

2. The Bioinformatics Root

My academic journey actually began in Biotechnology. From there, I naturally gravitated towards Bioinformatics, spending my time analyzing genomic sequences and molecular biology data.

Working with DNA, RNA, and protein structures was an incredible training ground. It taught me the critical importance of meticulous data cleaning and how to handle the sheer complexity of high-dimensional data spaces.

Tools of the trade: R, Python, SQL , Bash Scripting, Linux. (Scroll down to see the sidebar stick!)






3. Transition to Data Science

As I delved deeper into computational biology, my curiosity about the underlying algorithms grew. I wanted to truly understand the mathematical mechanics behind the models I was using and, more importantly, learn how to apply these methods to various types of data beyond just biology.

This drive to broaden my technical horizons led me to my current Master's program in Data Science. Now, I am expanding my toolkit to include mainstream predictive modeling, Natural Language Processing (NLP), and Deep Learning to tackle a much wider array of data challenges.

Current tech stack: Python, Pandas, Scikit-learn, PyTorch, SQL.
